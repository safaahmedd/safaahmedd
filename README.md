I paint. I read. I write. I like physical things in a world that keeps moving everything online.

I want to open a bakery. Not just any bakery — one designed **especially for older people**: somewhere warm, familiar, comfortable, and intentionally made for people who often get overlooked when we talk about "modern" spaces.

I don't know exactly what it'll look like yet. But I like having ideas that exist far beyond my current skill set. Maybe that's the point.
