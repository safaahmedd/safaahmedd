### 🌷 a little about me

Once upon a time (well, currently), I'm a second-year Data Science student who never quite picked a lane — I like the messy overlap where tech meets creativity meets *people*. I've sat in embassy meeting rooms, hackathon halls, and quiet corners with a beading needle, and somehow they all feel like the same kind of curious.

### 🗺️ my little adventures so far

> *🏛️ once, I was a Public Diplomacy Intern at the U.S. Embassy Islamabad — I helped run cultural programs, sketched out a website for Lincoln Corners, and got to sit in rooms with real diplomats, feeling very small and very lucky.*

> *🏆 once, my team and I placed as regional finalists in the Uber Global Hackathon, out of 730+ teams — still can't quite believe that one.*

> *🧠 and this past summer, I fell down an AI rabbit hole — a job simulation with Deloitte, an agentic AI workshop at a hackathon, and five days at an AI Impact camp hosted by the embassy. not the algorithms I study in class, but AI as something you actually build with.*

> *🎨 somewhere in there, I also picked up UX design (thank you, Google), because I think how something looks and feels matters just as much as how it works.*
